# The Art of Magic

**Platform:** Mobile
**Genre:** Deck‑building Roguelike
**Status:** Released prototype / Showcase project

---

## Overview

**The Art of Magic** is a mobile deck‑building roguelike focused on a distinctive spellcasting mechanic. Instead of activating abilities through buttons or cards alone, spells are cast by **drawing glyphs directly on the screen**, which are validated through a **shape‑recognition system**.

The project blends traditional roguelike progression with an interaction model that emphasizes player skill, precision, and learning patterns over time.

---

## Key Features

* Deck‑building roguelike structure with progressive runs
* Spellcasting based on **on‑screen glyph recognition**
* Drawing accuracy and speed directly affect gameplay
* Mobile‑first interaction design

---

## Technical Focus

* Design and implementation of a **glyph / shape recognition system** for spell validation
* Integration of gesture‑based input into core combat mechanics
* Balancing traditional deck‑building systems with real‑time player input
